1. About Brillare Scholars

Brillare Scholars ("Brillare," "we," "us," or "our") is a student-led educational initiative based in Chicago, Illinois, United States. We provide free educational resources, presentations, and workshops designed to introduce elementary and middle school students to real estate, business, finance, and related subjects.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of our students, volunteers, educators, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to individuals regarding their personal information.

Brillare Scholars is not currently incorporated or recognized as a tax-exempt nonprofit organization.

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at brillarescholars@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Student Portal and former registrations

Anyone can open the current Student Portal and use its core and bonus modules without registering, entering a name or email address, or submitting completed work. The current portal does not ask visitors for their age or track individual learning progress.

Our former Wix Student Portal asked registrants for their first name, last name and chosen email address. It did not ask their age. Brillare Scholars still has access to the former Wix site account. We retain a historical count of registrations. That count measures sign-ups only.

Volunteer applications

Both Google Forms ask for your full name, email address, grade, academic interests and reasons for applying. They ask you to confirm that you are at least 13, have read the Privacy Policy and agree to the Terms of Service.

The Join a Chapter application asks which chapter’s classroom visits you can attend. Providing your school is optional. The Start a Chapter application asks for your school, the city and state your proposed chapter would serve, your leadership experience and whether other students might help. You can add an optional comment. Please leave sensitive information and other students’ names and contact details out of your answers. Neither form accepts résumé uploads.

School outreach, visits and volunteer records

To arrange a visit, we use the host’s name, school and professional contact information, relevant correspondence, presenter names, scheduling details, materials and visitor arrangements. A teacher or designated school staff member confirms attendance. The Teaching Tracker records the school, supervising teacher or staff member, grade level, Presentation, Workshop, date and total attendance for each visit. Volunteers must leave classroom students’ names and contact details out of the tracker and visit recap.

The Student Educator Tracker lists accepted volunteers by name, role and chapter, including chapter leadership. We use contact details to arrange onboarding and rehearsals. We may receive a visit recap, chapter recognition and appointment details, or volunteer service records when an educator requests verification of hours. If you contact Brillare Scholars by email, we may retain your email address, correspondence, and any information you voluntarily provide so that we can respond to your inquiry or coordinate our activities.

Website and resource delivery

GitHub Pages hosts our website and may process IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure it. Pages that load Google Fonts send requests to Google. Opening a Google presentation, a form or a resource hosted on Wix infrastructure also connects your browser to the relevant provider. A provider may receive technical details such as your IP address and browser information when it delivers the resource.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Reviewing applications, selecting Pilot Leads, coordinating Student Educators and reviewing Pilots for chapter recognition.
  • Communicating with volunteers, educators, schools, and other interested individuals.
  • Organizing presentations, workshops, and educational initiatives.
  • Maintaining basic program records and measuring the number of students reached.
  • Improving our educational materials, programs, and outreach.
  • Responding to inquiries, requests, and concerns.
  • Maintaining the security and operation of our website and services.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We may publish aggregated statistics, such as classroom attendance across visits, former portal registrations, Workshops delivered or volunteers involved.

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties, and we do not use personal information collected through our forms for targeted advertising.

4. Children's and Minors' Privacy

Brillare Scholars provides educational resources and workshops primarily for elementary and middle school students, including children under the age of 13.

We are committed to protecting children's privacy and minimizing the personal information collected through our educational programs.

Students and Educational Participants

Our Student Portal and educational materials are publicly accessible without registration or the creation of an account.

We do not require students to provide their names, email addresses, or other personal information to access our educational content.

For classroom visits, we record the visit details listed in Section 2. We do not routinely collect or maintain individual student attendance lists, academic grades, or personal learning records.

Student Educators and Volunteers

Individuals must be at least 13 years old to apply to become a Student Educator, Chapter President, or other volunteer through either application.

Applicants under 18 are encouraged to review the application with a parent or legal guardian and must obtain any authorization required by applicable law or the relevant school or program.

Information submitted through volunteer applications is used for recruitment, communication, and program coordination, as described in this Privacy Policy.

Parental Rights and Inquiries

Parents and legal guardians may contact Brillare Scholars to ask whether we hold personal information concerning their child and to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

If you believe a child under 13 submitted a volunteer application or sent personal information online, please contact us so we can locate the information and arrange deletion as appropriate. We review parental requests in accordance with applicable law.

For questions or concerns about children's privacy, contact brillarescholars@gmail.com.

5. Information Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell personal information.

We may share limited information when reasonably necessary to operate Brillare Scholars, including with the following parties:

Google: We use Google Forms to receive volunteer applications and Google services to manage certain communications, documents, and organizational records. Information submitted through these services is processed according to Google's applicable policies and service terms.

Website Infrastructure Providers: Our website is developed and maintained using GitHub and delivered through web hosting infrastructure. Relevant providers may process technical information necessary to operate and secure the website.

Application review and chapter coordination. The Executive Team reviews Start a Chapter applications. When you apply to join a Pilot, the Pilot Lead or an appointed Chapter Outreach Lead may read your full Join a Chapter response, including your contact information and written answers. Once the chapter is recognized, the Chapter President or appointed Chapter Outreach Lead may read full responses from applicants to that chapter and contact them about the outcome. We share volunteer and host contact details as needed for onboarding, rehearsals, school visits and service-hour verification.

Educational Partners: Where necessary for a particular program, limited information may be shared with relevant teachers, schools, or partner organizations for coordination and administration.

Legal and Safety Requirements: We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to address serious safety or security concerns.

We seek to limit disclosures to the information necessary for their intended purposes.

6. Data Storage and Security

Personal information collected through volunteer applications and email communications is maintained using the relevant Google services and associated accounts.

We take reasonable steps to protect the information under our control against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. We seek to limit access to individuals who require the information for legitimate organizational purposes.

Our publicly accessible educational portal does not require individual student accounts or passwords.

Although we take privacy and security seriously, no method of electronic communication or data storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Former Wix registrations

Former registrants and their parents or guardians may contact us about registration information that may remain in Wix, including requests for access or deletion. We will check what records remain and respond in accordance with applicable law.

Volunteer Applications and Organizational Records

Volunteer application information, including names, email addresses, educational information, application responses, and chapter preferences, is retained for recruitment, communication, and program administration.

We review application records periodically and delete or anonymize information that is no longer necessary. Records relating to unsuccessful applicants and former volunteers are reviewed at least annually.

Certain records may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary for legitimate organizational purposes or compliance with applicable legal obligations.

Workshop and Educational Program Records

We maintain basic records of educational presentations and workshops, including educator names, session dates, and total student participation counts.

These records support program coordination, historical reporting, and measurement of our educational impact. We do not maintain individual student attendance or academic progress records.

Aggregated Impact Statistics

Aggregated statistics that do not identify individual students, such as classroom attendance across visits, former portal registrations, Workshops delivered or volunteers involved, may be retained indefinitely for organizational and educational reporting.

Requests for Deletion

Individuals may request deletion of their personal information by contacting brillarescholars@gmail.com. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

8. Photographs, Media, and Public Communications

Brillare Scholars may publish photographs or other media documenting educational workshops, volunteer activities, classroom presentations, and organizational initiatives on our website, LinkedIn page, or other official communication channels.

We recognize that photographs and recordings may contain identifiable individuals, including minors.

Volunteers must obtain appropriate authorization before taking or sharing identifiable photos or recordings of classroom students. They must follow the host school’s procedures and obtain any required parent or guardian permission for the intended use. We also seek appropriate permission before publishing identifiable volunteer media or featuring a student’s original work.

Participation in a Brillare Scholars workshop does not automatically constitute consent to photography or publication.

Photographs and other media are used to document our educational activities, communicate our mission, and highlight the contributions of our volunteers and participating communities.

Individuals, parents, or legal guardians who have questions or concerns about published photographs or other identifying material may contact us at brillarescholars@gmail.com to request review or removal.

We will evaluate such requests promptly and remove material where appropriate or legally required.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request to:

  • Know whether we maintain personal information about them.
  • Access or obtain a copy of personal information we hold.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for particular uses of information where processing depends on consent.
  • Ask us to stop sending optional communications.

Parents and legal guardians may submit applicable requests on behalf of their children.

To make a request, email brillarescholars@gmail.com.

We may request reasonable information to verify identity or parental authority before disclosing or deleting personal records.

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. Where relevant privacy laws provide additional rights, including complaint or appeal rights, those rights remain unaffected.

10. Cookies and Website Analytics

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services may store on a visitor's device to support website functionality, maintain preferences, or collect usage information.

Brillare Scholars does not currently use its own analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or behavioral tracking tools.

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages, which may process technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, for security and operational purposes.

External services linked through our website, including Google Forms and LinkedIn, may use cookies or other technologies according to their own privacy policies.

Visitors may manage or restrict cookies through their browser settings.

11. External Links and Services

Our website may link to third-party websites or services, including Google resources and LinkedIn.

These external services operate independently and may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Brillare Scholars does not control the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We encourage users and parents to review the policies of external services before providing personal information.

12. International Visitors

Brillare Scholars is based in the United States, and information submitted to our organization may be processed or stored through service providers operating in the United States or other countries.

If our programs expand internationally, we will review applicable privacy requirements and update our practices and disclosures where necessary.

Individuals located outside the United States may have additional rights under the privacy laws of their jurisdiction.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our programs, website functionality, data practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Where required by law, or where significant changes materially affect how we handle personal information, we will provide additional notice or obtain any necessary consent.
