2. About Brillare Scholars

Brillare Scholars is an educational initiative dedicated to introducing young people to real estate, business, finance, and related subjects.

Our mission is to make foundational education in these fields accessible, engaging, and practical for students.

We are a student-led initiative and are not currently incorporated or recognized as a tax-exempt nonprofit organization.

10. Volunteers, Schools, and Partnerships

Starting a Pilot and recognizing a chapter

The Executive Team reviews Start a Chapter applications. A selected applicant begins as Pilot Lead and may recruit Student Educators, appoint an accepted Student Educator as Chapter Outreach Lead, and contact potential school hosts for the Pilot. The Pilot Lead organizes and hosts the first classroom visit with the Student Educators. Afterward, the Pilot Lead sends a recap to the Executive Team. The Executive Team reviews how the visit went and decides whether to recognize the chapter and appoint the Pilot Lead as Chapter President. If approved, the Executive Team confirms both decisions by email.

Joining and leading a chapter

Prospective Student Educators apply through Join a Chapter. During a Pilot, the Pilot Lead handles school outreach and may appoint an accepted Student Educator as Chapter Outreach Lead. The Pilot Lead or appointed Chapter Outreach Lead may review full application responses from prospective Student Educators for that Pilot. After recognition, the Chapter President handles school outreach and may appoint a Chapter Outreach Lead. The Chapter President or appointed Chapter Outreach Lead reviews local applications and contacts applicants about the outcome. At either stage, the leader making the appointment notifies the Executive Team and updates the roster once the educator accepts. The Chapter Outreach Lead role is optional.

Classroom visits and volunteer responsibilities

Arrange classroom visits through a teacher, administrator or designated school contact. A teacher or designated school staff member must remain present throughout the Presentation and Workshop. Student Educators must prepare with approved materials, follow school visitor and safety procedures and respect the privacy of all participants. Ask the host to confirm total attendance. Keep classroom students’ names and contact details out of visit records and public descriptions.

Student Educators record their service hours according to their school’s requirements where applicable. The Chapter President coordinates verification. On a résumé, LinkedIn or in school communications, use the title you hold for the dates you held it. Describe your own contributions and clearly label any chapter-wide figures.

Volunteers may use Brillare Scholars’ name and materials within their authorized role. Official chapter or partnership status requires authorization. The Executive Team may withdraw authorization to protect participants, maintain teaching standards or address misuse. Host institutions may impose additional requirements for their programs.
